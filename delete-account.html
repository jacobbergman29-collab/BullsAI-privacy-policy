Delete Your Mr. Bullseye Account

You can permanently delete your Mr. Bullseye account directly from the app. This will remove your account and all associated data.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Mr. Bullseye app
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen
  3. Tap the Delete Account button
  4. Confirm deletion in the dialog

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (phone number)
  • All locally stored session data
  • All app preferences and cached data

Timing

All data is deleted immediately upon confirmation. There is no retention period.

If you have trouble deleting your account, contact us at support@thebullsaiproject.com
