Delete Your Mr. Bullseye Data

You can request deletion of your data stored by Mr. Bullseye. Since all data is stored locally on your device and tied to your account, deleting your account will also delete all your data.

How to delete your data

  1. Open the Mr. Bullseye app
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen
  3. Tap the Delete Account button
  4. Confirm deletion in the dialog

Data that is deleted

  • Account data — Your phone number and Firebase Authentication record
  • Session data — All shooting session history stored in the local database
  • Preferences — All app settings and cached preferences

Data we do NOT store on servers

Mr. Bullseye does not store personal data on remote servers. All session data, photos, and detection results are processed and stored locally on your device only. The only server-side data is your Firebase Authentication record (phone number), which is deleted when you delete your account.

Timing

All data is deleted immediately upon confirmation. There is no retention period or delayed deletion.

If you need assistance with data deletion, contact us at support@thebullsaiproject.com
