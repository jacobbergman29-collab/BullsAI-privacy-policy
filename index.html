BullsAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 4, 2026

BullsAI ("we", "our", or "us") operates the BullsAI mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Phone number - Used for account creation and authentication via SMS verification.

User Preferences

  • Skill level, preferred weapon type, training goals, training frequency, and play style. This information is collected during onboarding to personalize your experience.

Training & Session Data

  • Training session results, performance metrics, and scores generated while using the App. This data is stored locally on your device.

Camera Data

  • The App uses your device camera in real-time for target and bullet hole detection. Camera images are processed on-device and are not stored, uploaded, or shared.

Usage Analytics

  • We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data such as app opens, feature usage, and crash reports to improve the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To personalize your training experience based on your preferences.
  • To track and display your training progress and session history.
  • To improve the App through anonymous usage analytics.
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues via crash reports.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Authentication - For phone number verification and user authentication. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics - For anonymous usage analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics - For crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services - For app integrity and security. Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Training session data and user preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Account authentication is handled securely through Firebase.
  • Camera images are processed in real-time on your device and are never transmitted or stored.
  • We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as described in the Third-Party Services section above, which are necessary for the App to function.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your account and associated data by contacting us.
  • Clear locally stored training data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.
  • Opt out of analytics by disabling usage data sharing in your device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
